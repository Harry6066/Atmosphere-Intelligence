Scientific Analysis: Why Do Isolation Forest and LOF Disagree?

INT396 Comparative Synthesis
1
Geometric vs Density Topology

Isolation Forest recursively cuts hyperplanes orthogonal to coordinate axes. Observations at the outer extremities of the 10-dimensional space are isolated rapidly via short paths.

Conversely, LOF operates strictly in local density ratios within k=20 nearest neighbors. An observation situated in a moderately sparse neighborhood can be flagged even if its absolute coordinates are near the global center.

2
Polar Vortex vs Tropical Clusters

During winter cold snaps in Chicago (e.g. the January 2019 Polar Vortex at -30.6°C), observations become extreme across multiple dimensions (TMAX, TMIN, diurnal swing, snow depth). These trigger Isolation Forest globally (227 IF anomalies in Chicago).

In contrast, Miami features a compact, high-density cluster of tropical observations. A localized deviation in Miami (87 LOF anomalies vs only 7 IF) disturbs local neighbor ratios without reaching extreme outer values in global space.

3
The Binary Agreement Paradox

Students frequently confuse 95.33% Binary Agreement with model equivalence. In highly imbalanced anomaly detection (3% contamination), normal instances constitute 97% of the dataset.

The two algorithms agree on 11,064 normal observations: Agreement = (11,064 + 78) / 11,688 = 95.33% However, among candidate anomalies, the Jaccard index is only 0.1250 (78 / 624). This confirms they capture orthogonal physical characteristics of weather deviations.
