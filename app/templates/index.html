Scientific Interpretation & Meteorological Ground Truth

Critical distinction between statistical outliers and confirmed hardware sensor malfunctions.

Why Compare Two Methods?

Isolation Forest isolates points globally using random hyperplanes, excelling at extreme value detection. In contrast, LOF measures local neighborhood density, identifying points isolated relative to surrounding clusters. Comparing both guards against individual algorithmic bias.

What Consensus Represents

The 78 consensus anomalies represent observation records flagged by both Isolation Forest and LOF independently. These points exhibit high multi-dimensional divergence from normal seasonal distributions and warrant immediate quality inspection.

Outliers != Faulty Sensors

Crucial Scientific Principle: Statistical outliers are NOT automatically broken sensors. Genuine severe weather events (e.g. flash floods, blizzards, heat waves) naturally produce extreme measurements that are meteorologically valid despite statistical rarity.

Historical Benchmarks

Verified historical events confirm detection efficacy: the 2019 Polar Vortex (-30.6°C Chicago), the 2021 Pacific NW Heat Dome (42.2°C Seattle), and Hurricane Ida (181.1mm Central Park) were all successfully captured.

Academic Evaluation Note: The 78 consensus records represent candidate anomalies for human-in-the-loop meteorological audit rather than confirmed hardware failures. Physical rule checks confirmed 0 inverted temperatures (TMIN > TMAX) and 0 negative precipitation values, demonstrating that the NOAA GHCN-Daily quality screening successfully filtered gross corruptions before ingest.
