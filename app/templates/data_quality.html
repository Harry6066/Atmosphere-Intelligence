Why StandardScaler was Selected: Local Outlier Factor (LOF) relies on Euclidean distances, and PCA maximizes variance along orthogonal axes. StandardScaler guarantees equal geometric weighting across all 10 feature axes.

Scientific Interpretation: The Limits of Preprocessing

Crucial academic distinctions between clean data formatting and ground-truth sensor accuracy.

Clean != Accurate

A dataset with 0.0% missingness and zero physical rule violations is clean in format, but NOT guaranteed to be free of faulty readings. Subtle sensor drift (+3°C calibration error) or micro-site thermal biases easily pass coarse physical boundary checks.

Never Prune Extremes

Standard data cleaning in generic data science often trims or clips the top 1% of outliers. In weather anomaly detection, doing so would erase the exact target events (e.g. Polar Vortex or Heat Domes) that unsupervised models must evaluate.

Trace Precipitation

The 1,352 trace precipitation days represent genuine sub-millimeter condensation. Conflating trace with missing data corrupts rain occurrence rates, while setting trace to large values distorts annual precipitation totals.

Preprocessing Bias

Every preprocessing decision introduces an inductive bias. For instance, forward-filling temperatures preserves autocorrelation, while standard scaling implicitly assumes Gaussian-like distributions for distance calculations.
