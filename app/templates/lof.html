Local Outlier Factor (LOF) Anomaly Detection

Estimates local density reachability against k-nearest neighbors. Identifies observations isolated relative to their local climatological neighborhood rather than the global distribution.

Isolation Forest Neighborhood Sensitivity

Algorithm Fundamentals: Local Density vs Global Isolation

Breunig et al. (SIGMOD 2000)
1 Local Density Reachability

Global algorithms fail when clusters have different densities. Miami forms a dense, compact cluster; Chicago forms a wide, dispersed cluster. LOF compares an observation’s density solely to its k nearest neighbors.

2 Reachability Distance & Ratio

reach-dist_k(p, o) = max(k-dist(o), d(p, o)). The local reachability density lrd(p) is inverted average reachability. LOF is the average ratio of neighbors’ lrds to the observation’s own lrd.

3 Scikit-Learn Output Convention

Scikit-learn defines negative_outlier_factor_ = -LOF. Normal points score near -1.0. Points scoring significantly below the decision threshold offset_ ≈ -1.363 are classified as outliers.

Academic Defense: Why LOF Detects Tropical Weather Outliers

1. Local vs Global Geometry: Isolation Forest flagged only 7 anomalies in Miami because tropical temperatures lie within the global range. LOF flags 87 anomalies in Miami because Miami has a dense cluster where even minor departures (e.g. rare cool nights or storms) create low local reachability densities.

2. Sensitivity to Scale k: Small k focuses on micro-clusters (detecting localized variations), while larger k considers wider neighborhoods. Neither setting implies ground-truth sensor fault without physical inspection.
